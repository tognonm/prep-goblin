# 🎲 Demo Table — Quick Prompts and Encounters

> [!TIP] Triple Compatibility
> This starter table works out of the box with:
> 1. **Obsidian Dice Roller** (click the inline roll buttons below);
> 2. **Atlas VTT `0.16.0` `randomTables`** (registered in `atlas-vtt/collections/Campaign/collection.json`);
> 3. **CLI `live-improv`** (`python .agents/skills/live-improv/scripts/roll_tables.py --preset demo-scene`).

## 3. Quick Road Encounter or Complication

Dice Roller: `dice: [[Demo Table - Quick Prompts and Encounters#^demo-encounter]]`

| d6 | Event / Complication | Immediate Effect or Choice |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Sudden river mist** | Visibility drops to `30 ft.` for 10 minutes; ideal cover for an ambush or stealthy bypass |
| 2 | **Suspicious local patrol** | Demands travel papers or business; Charisma **DC 12** to pass without delay |
| 3 | **Overturned cart on the track** | Blocks the lane; helping the driver right it earns a local ally and a useful rumor |
| 4 | **Two [[Road Raider\|Road Raiders]] lying in wait** | Watch travelers from behind cover; retreat quickly if spotted before springing their trap |
| 5 | **Breathless courier** | Asks urgent directions and accidentally drops a ribbon-tied dispatch |
| 6 | **Distant alarm bell** | Someone is ringing for help in the opposite direction from the party's current goal |
^demo-encounter
