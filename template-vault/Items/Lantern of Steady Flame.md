# 🏮 Lantern of Steady Flame

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

Forged from tarnished brass with faceted amber panes, this storm-shuttered lantern burns with a quiet, smokeless flame that never gutters in wind, rain, or subterranean drafts.

## Properties
- **Steady Illumination:** Speaking the lantern's command word ignites or snuffs the flame without oil. While lit, it sheds **bright light in a 30-foot radius** and **dim light for an additional 30 feet**.
- **Shuttered Baffles:** As a bonus action, the bearer can slide the brass shutters closed to narrow the glow to **dim light in a 5-foot radius**, or open them again to restore full light.
- **Draft-Proof Wick:** Nonmagical gusts, mist, and heavy rain cannot extinguish the flame; full submersion suppresses the light until the lantern resurfaces.

## Provenance
- Crafted by an itinerant borderlands tinsmith and acquired by [[Mira the Cleric|Mira]] before the party passed through [[Oakhaven Village]].
