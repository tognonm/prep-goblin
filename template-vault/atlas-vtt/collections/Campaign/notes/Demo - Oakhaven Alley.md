# Demo - Oakhaven Alley

Cobblestone street with a T-junction alley, crate cover, streetlamps, and a two-door side warehouse.

- **Dimensions:** `16x12` cells (`80 ft x 60 ft`, `5 ft` per square).
- **Lighting:** Dusk (`ambient: 0.35`, Mira carrying torch and ambient lights active).
- **PC Tokens:** [[Mira the Cleric|Mira]], [[Kael the Rogue|Kael]], [[Bram the Fighter|Bram]] ready on the grid.
- **Party Passives (Lv. 3):** **Mira** Perc. `13` / Inv. `10` / Ins. `15` | **Kael** Perc. `11` / Inv. `14` / Ins. `10` | **Bram** Perc. `10` / Inv. `9` / Ins. `12`.

---

### A. Cobblestone Street (West Entry)
- Main street (`20 ft` wide) lit by two oil streetlamps (`bright 15 ft / dim 30 ft` at dusk and night).
- Crates and barrels in the southern loading alcove and along the curb provide **half cover (`+2 AC`)**.

### B. Side Alley and T-Junction
- Narrow side alley (`15 ft` wide), ideal for ambushes, flanking maneuvers, or a northward escape.
- ⚡ `[Automatic Passive — Mira (Perc. 13)]` Immediately spots stacked crates at the northwest corner of the junction offering ideal cover for a ranged attacker.

### C. Side Warehouse (Two Doors)
- Accessible via **two closed unlocked doors** (one facing the main street, one opening onto the side alley).
- Clicking either door in Atlas VTT immediately updates dynamic line of sight and light propagation for all players.
- 🎲 `[Active Roll — Perception or Investigation DC 14]` To search the stacked crates in the northeast corner of the warehouse.
