# 📌 Demo Quest — The Missing Wagon

> [!NOTE] Atlas VTT `0.16.0` Native Quest Integration

## Hook
A supply wagon bound for [[Oakhaven Village]] never arrived at the square; fresh mud tracks trail off toward the old east market lane ([[Demo - Oakhaven Alley]]).

## Objectives
- [ ] Question eyewitnesses in the village square.
- [ ] Inspect the east alley and its two-door side warehouse.
- [ ] Recover the sealed cargo crates or unmask the culprits.

> [!danger] DM Secret
> Example isolated secret: one of the crates carries rare northern spices promised to a rival broker. Replace this demo quest freely with your own campaign hooks.
